Our mission

The FKF Meru County League promotes football development at the grassroots level in Meru County. We provide a structured league system that nurtures talent, promotes fair play, and creates opportunities for players to showcase their skills.

League structure

The league is organised into zones. Each team plays home and away matches within its zone, with the top teams qualifying for inter-zonal playoffs at the end of the season.

See the current standings or browse all teams.

Registration process

  1. Complete the team registration form online.
  2. Add player details (minimum 15, maximum 30 players).
  3. Pay the registration fee via M-Pesa.
  4. Wait for admin approval and zone assignment.
  5. Receive fixtures and start playing.
