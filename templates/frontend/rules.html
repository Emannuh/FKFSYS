General rules

  1. All matches shall be played in accordance with FIFA Laws of the Game.
  2. Each team must register a minimum of 15 players and a maximum of 30 players.
  3. All players must be registered with the league before participating in any match.
  4. The team registration fee is payable via M-Pesa.
  5. Matches shall be played as per the published fixture list.

Match rules

  • Matches last 90 minutes (two halves of 45 minutes).
  • Each team must have at least 7 players to start a match.
  • A maximum of 5 substitutions per match, all made through the fourth official.
  • Teams must report to the match venue at least 2 hours before kick-off.
